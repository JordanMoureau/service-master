# ServiceMaster by Compass

Ongoing SEO strategy, content development, technical optimization, and conversion work for ServiceMaster by Compass, a fire, water, mold, and disaster restoration company serving the Spokane region.

I began working with ServiceMaster by Compass after the company transitioned away from a national SEO agency. Over the following year, I developed and executed a long-term organic search strategy focused on technical SEO, content architecture, service-page expansion, internal linking, backlink development, local search relevance, and conversion performance.

## SEO Performance

Search visibility grew substantially over the course of the campaign.

From the first 28 days represented in Search Console data to the most recent 28-day period:

- Organic clicks increased from **6 to 55**
- Organic impressions increased from **925 to 22,736**
- Average search position improved from approximately **26.4 to 14.9**
- Search visibility expanded from under 1,000 impressions per month-equivalent period to more than 22,000

Across the available reporting period from October 2025 through September 2026, the site generated:

- **123,000+ organic impressions**
- **460 organic search clicks**
- Visibility across hundreds of relevant search queries
- Strong growth in both branded and non-branded search presence

Several individual months produced especially large gains as new content and optimization strategies were indexed.

One 28-day reporting period saw organic impressions increase from approximately **6,190 to 17,500**, representing roughly **183% month-over-month growth**, while clicks increased approximately **64%**.

Later campaign reporting showed search appearances increasing again from approximately **13,000 to 26,000 in a single month**.

## Strategy

The campaign has included a combination of technical, content, authority, and conversion-focused SEO work.

### Technical SEO

Ongoing technical work has included:

- Full-site SEO auditing
- Metadata and title optimization
- Crawl and indexing review
- Internal-link architecture
- Search Console monitoring
- GA4 performance analysis
- Click-through-rate optimization
- Page hierarchy and content-tree development
- Local relevance optimization

## Content Strategy

A major focus of the campaign has been expanding ServiceMaster's authority around high-intent restoration searches.

New and expanded service content has included topics related to:

- Water damage restoration
- Water mitigation
- Flood damage
- Burst pipes
- Sewage backup
- Fire-related flooding
- Mold remediation
- Commercial storm damage restoration
- Emergency restoration services

Rather than publishing isolated pages, new content has been organized into topical clusters and deeply interlinked with established service pages.

This approach helps strengthen topical relationships between pages and gives search engines clearer signals about the site's authority across related restoration services.

## Internal Linking

Internal linking became a major focus as the site accumulated more service-specific content.

New pages were nested beneath core service categories and connected to related existing content throughout the site.

This created stronger content pathways for both users and search crawlers while allowing authority from established pages to flow into newly published service pages.

## Backlink Strategy

Backlink campaigns have been deployed periodically throughout the engagement.

Strategies have included both:

- Local authority and geographic relevance campaigns
- Niche-specific authority campaigns designed around restoration and emergency-service topics

Because backlink gains typically take time to influence rankings, performance has been monitored over subsequent 60–90 day windows rather than evaluated immediately after deployment.
